# 0014. Client portal

**Date**: 2026-09-16
**Status**: Proposed

## Summary

This spec is the build plan for the client's own window onto their work: a signed in contact opens `/portal` and sees their projects, the files the agency chose to share, and their invoices, and nothing else. It is strictly read only: the only thing a contact can change is which client they are looking at, when one person is a contact of several. Every page resolves who the contact is from their session and their `client_contacts` row, never from the URL, and every read goes through the contact accessor spec 0003 built, so a contact of one client cannot reach another client's rows by construction. A contact whose agency has stopped paying sees a plain "not available" page that names the agency and says nothing about money. The chrome (the frame around the pages) is the one spec 0004 already wrote down: a top bar, three sections, no sidebar, no primary buttons.

## Requirements

**User stories**:
- As a client contact, I want to open one place and see what my agency is working on for me, what they have shared, and what I owe, so that I do not have to ask by email.
- As a client contact, I want to download a shared file or an invoice PDF from the same place, so that the link the agency emailed me and the portal agree.
- As a client contact who works with several agencies, or is the contact for several companies, I want to switch between them without signing out.
- As agency staff, I want my client to see only the projects, files and invoices I meant them to see, and to be certain another client can never see them.
- As agency staff whose account has lapsed, I want my clients to see a neutral page rather than my billing state.

**Acceptance criteria** (the contract, each criterion is IDed and independently checkable):

- **AC-1**: Every page under `/portal` except `/portal/accept` resolves the acting person through one request cached helper, `portalContext()` in `src/portal/context.ts`, which calls `tenantContext()` (spec 0003) and then: a staff context (an organization claim is present) is redirected to `/dashboard`; a `no_contact` resolution (the person has no accepted `client_contacts` row) is redirected to `/onboarding`; a contact context continues; any other resolution error (`no_mirror_row`, or a failure reaching the database) is not caught and falls through to the portal's `error.tsx` of AC-15. The signed out case never reaches the helper, because the proxy already requires a session on `/portal` (spec 0005, AC-20). No page, layout or route in the portal reads a client id, contact id or organization id from a URL segment, a search param, a form field or a header.
- **AC-2**: `portalContext()` then reads the agency's access level through `portalAccess(ctx)` in `src/portal/gate.ts`: an `unsafeTenantQuery` with the reason `portal gate` that selects `status` and `past_due_since` from `subscriptions` for `ctx.orgId` and nothing else, and hands the row to the shared `accessVerdict` from `src/access/level.ts`. `full` and `grace` are readable with no banner and no difference between them. `locked` and `unsubscribed` redirect to `/portal/unavailable`, a path held once in the constant `PORTAL_UNAVAILABLE_PATH` in `src/portal/gate.ts` that the helper and both file routes of AC-13 import, so the three cannot drift apart. Because every page calls the helper and the helper is cached per request, a lock is enforced on the very next page a contact navigates to, not only on a full page load. `/portal/unavailable` itself calls `portalContext()` without the redirect and sends a readable level back to `/portal`, so a restored agency's contact never gets stuck there.
- **AC-3**: `/portal/unavailable` renders, verbatim: heading `This portal is not available right now`, body `<Agency name>'s ClientHQ account needs attention before the portal can be shown. Contact <Agency name> if you need something in the meantime.`, a `Sign out` link, and the client switcher of AC-11 when the person has more than one accepted row. It names no billing, payment, subscription or trial state, and reads no table but `subscriptions` (the gate), `organizations` (the name) and the switcher's rows.
- **AC-4**: Every readable portal page shares one chrome from `src/portal/ui/portal-shell.tsx`: the skip link, then a top bar (a `banner` landmark) carrying the client's company name (`clients.name`, the switcher of AC-11 when there are several rows, otherwise plain text linking to `/portal`), the existing `ThemeControl` and the existing `UserMenu`; then a section strip (a `navigation` landmark labelled `Portal sections`) with exactly three links, `Projects` to `/portal/projects`, `Files` to `/portal/files`, `Invoices` to `/portal/invoices`, the current one marked with `aria-current="page"` (also on the section's detail pages); then the page content in a `main` landmark. There is no sidebar and no menu button at any width: the three links fit at 320 pixels and stay visible. No primary button renders anywhere in the portal. Every `<title>` is `<Page name> · <Client name> · ClientHQ`.
- **AC-5**: `/portal` is an overview of three blocks, each a labelled `section` with a heading and a `See all` link to its section: **Projects**, the first 5 rows of the AC-6 list in the AC-6 order, showing name (a link), status chip and due date; **Files**, the 5 most recently added shared files (the AC-8 rule and order), showing name (the download link), project name and the added date; **Invoices**, the unpaid invoices (`sent` and `overdue`) in the AC-9 order, up to 5, showing number (a link), due date, total and status chip. Each block has its own describing empty state: `No projects yet`, `No files shared yet`, `No invoices awaiting payment`, with the sentence `Your agency has not <started a project for you / shared any files with you / issued an invoice that is awaiting payment>.` and never a call to action.
- **AC-6**: `/portal/projects` lists the client's non archived projects in every status, ordered by due date soonest first, projects with no due date last, then by name, in pages of 25 with a `page` search param parsed by Zod (a missing, non numeric or out of range value reads as page 1). Each row shows the name (the one link, to `/portal/projects/[id]`), the status chip from `PROJECT_STATUS_PRESENTATION`, the due date, and the overdue badge computed by the existing `isOverdue` from `src/projects/status.ts` (spec 0010's rule: `due_date` before today, status not `delivered`, not archived). The order is a pure, unit tested comparator `compareProjectsForPortal` in `src/portal/queries.ts`, because `/projects` orders inline and exports nothing to share. Archived projects never appear. The empty state reads `No projects yet` with the sentence from AC-5.
- **AC-7**: `/portal/projects/[id]` shows the project's name, status chip, due date, overdue badge and description, then a `Files` section listing the files shared on that project under the AC-8 rule, newest first, each row the file name (the download link), type, size and added date, with the empty state `No files shared on this project yet`. An archived project, a project of another client (same agency or another), a nonexistent id and a non uuid all render the portal's not found page of AC-12 through `notFound()`.
- **AC-8**: `/portal/files` lists every deliverable that is `ready`, has `visible_to_client` true, and belongs to a non archived project of the contact's client, ordered by `created_at` newest first, in pages of 25 files with the same `page` parsing as AC-6; within a page, rows are grouped under a heading per project (the project name, a link to its AC-7 page) in the order the group's newest file appears. Each row shows the file name as the one link, to `/deliverables/[id]/download` (spec 0011, reused unchanged for the redirect), the type from `content_type`, the size through `formatBytes`, and the `created_at` date labelled `Added`. `pending` rows, hidden rows and rows on archived projects never appear. The empty state reads `No files shared yet` with the sentence from AC-5.
- **AC-9**: `/portal/invoices` lists the client's invoices whose status is in `CLIENT_VISIBLE_STATUSES` (spec 0012), unpaid first (`sent` and `overdue`) then `paid`, and within each group by issue date newest first then by number descending, in pages of 25 with the AC-6 `page` parsing. Each row shows the display number through `formatInvoiceNumber` (the one link, to `/portal/invoices/[id]`), issue date, due date, total through `formatMoney` in the invoice's currency, and the `InvoiceStatusChip` with the past due badge from `isPastDue`. Drafts and voided invoices never appear. The empty state reads `No invoices yet` with the sentence `Your agency has not issued an invoice to you.`
- **AC-10**: `/portal/invoices/[id]` renders the invoice through `getInvoiceDocument(ctx, id)` and `presentInvoice` (spec 0013), with the same `InvoiceDocument` component the agency page uses, so the screen, the agency's screen and the PDF carry identical strings; above the document, the status chip with the past due badge and the issued, due and (when paid) paid on lines; and a link whose accessible name is `Download PDF <display number>` to `/portal/invoices/[id]/pdf`. No events list and no notes from `invoice_events` are shown. A `draft`, a `void`, another client's invoice, a nonexistent id and a non uuid all render the not found page of AC-12 through `notFound()`. The spec 0012 placeholder at `src/app/portal/invoices/[id]/page.tsx` and the spec 0005 placeholder at `src/app/portal/page.tsx` are deleted, not extended.
- **AC-11**: A person with more than one accepted `client_contacts` row (across clients and across agencies) sees the top bar's company name as a menu button, `Switch client`, listing every row as `<Client name> · <Agency name>` with the current one marked, read through a named door `listAcceptedContactRows(userId)` in `src/db/tenant/contact-rows.ts` that takes the mirror row's id (`ctx.userId`, already resolved, so no join to `users`), selects `client_contacts` by `user_id` and `accepted_at is not null`, joins `clients.name` and `organizations.name`, and is the only read of `client_contacts` across organizations besides `src/db/tenant/context.ts`. Choosing a row calls the Server Action `switchContact({ contactId })` in `src/portal/switch-contact.ts` (outside `withTenantAction`, like `acceptInvitation`), which parses the id with Zod, refuses with `not_found` any id not in that person's rows, otherwise sets the `clienthq_contact` cookie with the attributes spec 0009 AC-10 fixed and redirects to `/portal`. With exactly one row the name is plain text and the action is never rendered. A refused switch writes one `console.warn` line `{ event: "portal.switch.refused", userId, at }` and the menu shows `That client is no longer available to you.`
- **AC-12**: `src/app/portal/(contact)/not-found.tsx` renders, inside the AC-4 chrome and with a real `404` status: heading `This page is not available`, body `It may have been removed or is no longer shared with you. Ask your agency if you expected to find it here.`, and a link `Back to your portal` to `/portal`. Every hidden, foreign, missing or malformed id in AC-7 and AC-10 lands here through `notFound()`, and the page is identical for all of them, so nothing reveals which case applied.
- **AC-13**: The contact branches of `GET /deliverables/[id]/download` (spec 0011) and `GET /portal/invoices/[id]/pdf` (spec 0013) run `portalAccess(ctx)` after resolving a contact context and before reading any row, and answer a `302` to `/portal/unavailable` when the level is `locked` or `unsubscribed`; `full` and `grace` serve as before. The staff branches of both routes are unchanged.
- **AC-14**: Every read in the portal goes through `tenantDb(ctx)` on the contact accessor, except three named doors: the gate of AC-2, the switcher rows of AC-11, and `agencyProfile(ctx)` from spec 0013 (the agency's name from `organizations` by `ctx.orgId`, which already carries its own cross tenant tests). A contact of client B of the same agency, and a contact of a client of another agency, see none of client A's projects, files or invoices on any list, overview block or detail page, and get the AC-12 page for every direct id; the portal performs no database write of any kind (no `last seen`, no view log), and the switcher's cookie is its only side effect. Database tests against real PostgreSQL prove each of these for every page's query.
- **AC-15**: Every portal page has a `loading.tsx` skeleton in the shape of its list or document, and `src/app/portal/(contact)/error.tsx` renders the spec 0004 error state pattern inside the chrome with a `Try again` button; the two are reused, not redrawn, from `src/ui/patterns/`. Empty, error, loading and every populated state of every portal page meet WCAG 2.2 AA: axe passes in both themes, every page is a single tab order with the skip link first, the switcher menu is keyboard operable and returns focus on close, dates are rendered in a `<time dateTime>` element, no page scrolls in two directions at 320 pixels, and the portal top bar, the section strip, the switcher and the three describing empty states are added to the `/design` gallery.
- **AC-16**: The contact path is driven by Playwright on a second Clerk development user, named by three new optional environment variables in `src/lib/env.ts` and `.env.example`: `E2E_CLERK_CONTACT_USERNAME`, `E2E_CLERK_CONTACT_PASSWORD` and `E2E_CLERK_CONTACT_USER_ID`. `scripts/db-seed.ts` writes `E2E_CLERK_CONTACT_USER_ID`, when set, as the `clerk_user_id` of the seeded contact Priya Patel (Northwind) so her accepted row binds to the real test account, and gains one client under the seeded past due agency (Harbor Lane) with one accepted `client_contacts` row for Priya's same `users` row, so the test user has two rows across two agencies; the suite skips, never fails, when any of the three is unset, exactly as the staff suite does with `E2E_CLERK_USER_*`. The walk signs in as that user, lands on the Northwind overview, opens each section and a project, follows one file download to a `302`, opens a `sent` invoice and its PDF, asserts the AC-12 page on another client's invoice id, then switches to the Harbor Lane row through the AC-11 menu and asserts the AC-3 page.
- **AC-17**: The feature adds no migration, no table, no column and no required environment variable. Every list is paged; no unpaged list exists in the portal.

## Decision

**Chosen option**: Option 1: Server rendered pages behind one cached `portalContext()` helper, a `(contact)` route group for the chrome, and a redirect based gate.

Each portal page is a Server Component that calls one request cached helper for its context and gate, reads its rows through the contact accessor, and renders inside a layout that owns the chrome; the gate redirects to a dedicated `/portal/unavailable` page instead of swapping content in the layout, so pages and layout never disagree, and the two existing file routes call the same gate function.

**Implementation skills**: `nextjs-v16` (`vercel-labs/agent-skills`, `.agents/skills/nextjs-v16/`) · `nextjs-app-router-patterns` (`.agents/skills/nextjs-app-router-patterns/`) · `vercel-react-best-practices` (`vercel-labs/agent-skills`, `.agents/skills/vercel-react-best-practices/`) · `typescript-core` (`.agents/skills/typescript-core/`) · `zod` (`.agents/skills/zod/`) · `shadcn` (`.agents/skills/shadcn/`) · `tailwind` (`.agents/skills/tailwind/`) · `accessibility-compliance` (`.agents/skills/accessibility-compliance/`) · `clerk-testing` (`.agents/skills/clerk-testing/`) · `vitest` (`.agents/skills/vitest/`) · `playwright-cli` (`.agents/skills/playwright-cli/`) · `drizzle` (`.agents/skills/drizzle/`)

## Feature design

**Data model sketch**:

No table, column or migration changes. The feature reads eight existing tables and writes nothing.

| Table | Scoped by (contact context) | Read | Written |
|---|---|---|---|
| `clients` | `id = ctx.clientId` | `name`; `archived_at` is not consulted | no |
| `client_contacts` | the AC-11 door: `user_id = users.id`, `accepted_at is not null`, across organizations | `id`, `client_id`, `org_id`, `accepted_at` | no |
| `users` | the AC-11 door, by `clerk_user_id` | `id` | no |
| `organizations` | `id = ctx.orgId`, and the AC-11 join | `name` | no |
| `subscriptions` | `unsafeTenantQuery`, reason `portal gate` | `status`, `past_due_since` | no |
| `projects` | `client_id = ctx.clientId`, `archived_at is null` | `id`, `name`, `status`, `due_date`, `description` | no |
| `deliverables` | own client's projects (spec 0003 predicate), `status = 'ready'`, `visible_to_client`, project not archived | `id`, `name`, `content_type`, `size_bytes`, `created_at`, `project_id` | no |
| `invoices`, `invoice_line_items` | `client_id = ctx.clientId`, status in `CLIENT_VISIBLE_STATUSES` | through `getInvoiceDocument` and the list columns of AC-9 | no |
| `invoice_events` | reachable, never read | none | no |

Relationships are unchanged: a client has many contacts, projects and invoices; a project has many deliverables; an invoice has many line items.

**State transitions**: none added. The portal reads three existing status enums and applies three existing rules: `archived_at is null` for projects (spec 0010), `ready` plus `visible_to_client` plus project not archived for deliverables (spec 0011), and `CLIENT_VISIBLE_STATUSES` for invoices (spec 0012). Each rule lives in one place already and is imported, never restated.

**Modules** (all under `src/portal/` unless named):

- `context.ts`: `portalContext()`, wrapped in React `cache()`: `tenantContext()`, the staff and `no_contact` redirects of AC-1, `portalAccess(ctx)`, the `locked` and `unsubscribed` redirect of AC-2, then `clientName` (through `tenantDb(ctx).findFirst(clients)`), `agencyName` (through `agencyProfile(ctx)`, spec 0013), and `contactRows` (the AC-11 door). Returns `{ ctx, access, clientName, agencyName, contactRows }`. A variant `portalContextForUnavailable()` runs the same steps without the gate redirect, for `/portal/unavailable` only.
- `gate.ts`: `portalAccess(ctx: ContactContext): Promise<AccessVerdict>`, the `unsafeTenantQuery` of AC-2 feeding `accessVerdict(row, new Date())`; an `invariantBreak` is reported through the existing `logGateInvariant`. `isPortalReadable(level)` is the pure rule: `full` and `grace` read, `locked` and `unsubscribed` do not, an exhaustive switch.
- `queries.ts`: `listPortalProjects(ctx, page)`, `getPortalProject(ctx, id)`, `listPortalFiles(ctx, page)`, `listProjectFiles(ctx, projectId)`, `listPortalInvoices(ctx, page)`, `overviewData(ctx)`, plus the pure `compareProjectsForPortal` comparator. Every function takes a `ContactContext` (not `TenantContext`), which makes a staff call a compile error, and every list returns `{ rows, page, pageCount, total }` in the shape the agency lists use, always as flat rows: the per project grouping of AC-8 is a presentation step the files page performs over the flat page of rows, not a query shape. `overviewData` calls the three list functions at page 1 and slices each to the cap (the invoices call with an `unpaidOnly` flag), so the overview and the sections cannot disagree on a rule. Paging runs in memory over one client's already filtered rows, as `listClients` and `listInvoices` do.
- `schema.ts`: the Zod schemas for the `page` search param and for the `id` route params (uuid), and for `switchContact`'s input.
- `copy.ts`: every pinned string of AC-3, AC-5, AC-7, AC-8, AC-9, AC-11 and AC-12 as named constants, the single place the pages, the unavailable page and the not found page read them from.
- `switch-contact.ts`: the AC-11 Server Action.
- `src/db/tenant/contact-rows.ts`: `listAcceptedContactRows(userId)`, the named door of AC-11, the same shape as `src/db/tenant/invitation.ts` (a read that derives its rows from the signed in user, never from the caller's input).
- `ui/portal-shell.tsx`, `ui/portal-top-bar.tsx`, `ui/section-nav.tsx`, `ui/client-switcher.tsx` (a client component using the shadcn dropdown menu), `ui/portal-empty-state.tsx` (a thin wrapper over `EmptyState` with no action slot), and one list or block component per section reusing `DataTable`, `StatusChip`, `PastDueBadge`, `InvoiceDocument` and the `Pagination` primitives.
- Routes: `src/app/portal/(contact)/layout.tsx` (the chrome; it calls `portalContext()` for the top bar), `page.tsx` (overview), `projects/page.tsx`, `projects/[id]/page.tsx`, `files/page.tsx`, `invoices/page.tsx`, `invoices/[id]/page.tsx`, `not-found.tsx`, `error.tsx`, one `loading.tsx` per page; `src/app/portal/unavailable/page.tsx` (outside the group, its own minimal frame with the top bar); `src/app/portal/accept/` unchanged and outside the group; `src/app/portal/invoices/[id]/pdf/route.ts` unchanged in place (the URL `/portal/invoices/[id]/pdf` and the page `/portal/invoices/[id]` are distinct paths, so the group and the route folder coexist).

**Key invariants**:
- A contact context never reaches a row outside `ctx.clientId`: every portal read runs on the contact accessor, whose narrowing map (spec 0003) applies the client predicate on every table, and the three named doors read `subscriptions` and `organizations` by `ctx.orgId` and `client_contacts` by the signed in user only.
- The portal writes nothing to the database. The cookie set by `switchContact` is its only side effect.
- The three visibility rules are imported from where they live (`archived_at`, `ready` plus `visible_to_client` plus project not archived, `CLIENT_VISIBLE_STATUSES`); no second copy of any of them exists in `src/portal/`.
- `isPortalReadable` is the single readable or unavailable rule, and every page, the unavailable page and both file routes consult it; there is no page that skips the gate.
- The four not found cases (hidden, foreign, missing, malformed) render one identical page with one identical status.
- No primary button and no write control renders under `/portal`; the switcher menu and the theme control are the only interactive controls beyond links.
- Every list is paged at 25 and every overview block is capped at 5.

**Security model**:
- Who: a signed in person with at least one accepted `client_contacts` row, acting as exactly one contact at a time, chosen by the verified cookie. A staff session (an organization claim) is not a portal user and is sent to `/dashboard`; the person who is both signs in with their contact account to use the portal (spec 0005 AC-6, a membership always wins). A person with no accepted row is sent to `/onboarding`.
- What: read only, the six contact reachable tables, narrowed to their own client by construction; `memberships` and `subscriptions` are unreachable from the contact accessor at compile time, and the gate's one `subscriptions` read selects two columns and is fenced by the `unsafeTenantQuery` reason.
- Gate: `full` and `grace` read; `locked` and `unsubscribed` see the AC-3 page and get a `302` from both file routes. Spec 0008 named only `locked`; this spec adds `unsubscribed` (no row, or a Checkout that never finished) to the unavailable side for the same reason, nothing is being paid for, and records that in Consequences.
- Switching: `switchContact` accepts only an id from the person's own accepted rows, so a forged id is `not_found` and the cookie is never set to it; the resolver's own fallback (spec 0003 AC-5) covers a cookie that names a row they no longer own.
- Data: the portal shows invoice figures, the client's billing address, file names and project descriptions, personal and business data already held and already shown to staff; no new data category, no card or bank data, no PCI DSS scope. Nothing in the portal is logged except the AC-11 refusal line, which carries a user id and never a client or an invoice.
- Abuse: every portal page sits behind a session; feature 19 adds the portal pages, the switch action and the two routes to its per contact ceilings. Nothing here depends on it.

**Configuration required**:
- `E2E_CLERK_CONTACT_USERNAME` (optional): the sign in name of the second Clerk development user the browser suite signs in as.
- `E2E_CLERK_CONTACT_PASSWORD` (optional): its password.
- `E2E_CLERK_CONTACT_USER_ID` (optional): that user's Clerk id (`user_…`), which the seed writes onto the seeded contact so the row binds to the real account. All three are declared in `src/lib/env.ts` and `.env.example`, are never read at runtime by the application, and the suite skips when any is unset. Prerequisite: create that user in the Clerk development instance (spec 0005's `e2e/CLERK.md` describes the instance) with the seeded contact's email or any verified email, and give it no agency membership.

## Consequences

**Positive**:
- Isolation is inherited, not built: every read runs on the contact accessor spec 0003 already fenced, so the tenancy tests here prove the plumbing rather than new predicates.
- One gate function, called from every page and both file routes, gives the portal a single readable or unavailable rule and closes the gap spec 0008 noted for layouts.
- The three views of an invoice (agency screen, PDF, portal) render from one presentation module, so they cannot drift.
- The front door is machine proven: the contact sign in path that spec 0009 walked by hand now runs in CI on every push.
- The portal writes nothing, so it adds no new failure mode to the database and nothing for GDPR erasure to scrub beyond what the contact row already carries.

**Negative / tradeoffs**:
- A person who is both agency staff and a client contact cannot use the portal from their staff account; they need a second sign in. A limitation to explain in the help text, not a bug.
- `Added` on a file is its upload date, not the moment it was shared, so a file uploaded a month ago and shared today sorts low. Adding `shared_at` later means a migration and a change to `setDeliverableVisibility`.
- One `subscriptions` read per portal request, on top of the context resolution; at this product's volume that is invisible, and both are request cached.
- The browser suite needs a second Clerk development user and three more environment variables in CI's secrets; without them the contact path silently skips, so someone must notice a skipped suite.
- The unavailable rule now covers `unsubscribed` as well as `locked`, which spec 0008 did not name; a contact of an agency that never finished Checkout, but somehow has contacts, sees the unavailable page rather than an empty portal.

**Neutral**:
- `src/app/portal/` gains a `(contact)` route group; `/portal/accept` and the PDF route stay outside it and are untouched.
- `src/db/tenant/` gains one more named door, `contact-rows.ts`, in the shape of `invitation.ts`; `src/db/AGENTS.md` should list it on the next `/sync`.
- When the cookie names a row that has since been removed, the resolver's fallback (spec 0003 AC-5) silently shows the person's most recently accepted other row; nothing leaks, but the portal does not say "you are now looking at a different client". A notice for that case is a follow up.
- `src/portal/` is a new feature folder alongside `src/invoices/` and `src/contacts/`; it owns no table.
- Every date the portal shows is a calendar day string rendered as the agency screens render it; there is still no per user timezone anywhere in the product.
