- **AC-2**: `portalAccess`/`isPortalReadable` unit tested over every level and proven against real seeded rows (`full` for Studio North, `grace` for Harbor Lane, `locked` for Anchor Ridge); the redirect walked signed in for real by switching to the locked row.
